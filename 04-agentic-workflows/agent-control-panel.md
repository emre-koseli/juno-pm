## What Juno Is Doing

Shop-licensing integration monitoring: Juno reads Teams channel XYZ, tracks each Dead Letter Queue (DLQ) failure, and reports its status to Emre without acting on any of them.

## Four Levers

**Stop Conditions** (Loop):

max_steps: 40 tool calls per run. A step is one tool call. A normal run uses 2 fixed reads (the channel and the tracking log) plus about 4 calls per open DLQ (two time calculations, draft the card, update the log). 40 covers roughly 9 open DLQs, plus the one allowed re-read of an unclear thread.

**Structured Tool Outputs** (Tools):

Rule for every tool: a result is a success only if it has status: "ok". A missing status, a null count, or an empty reply is a failure, never "nothing to report."

read_channel: success is {status: "ok", messages, count, complete: true}. Failure is {status: "error", count: null} or complete: false (a gap). A count of 0 with complete: true is a quiet period. A count of 0 with complete: false is a failure.
read_tracking_log: success is {status: "ok", cards, count}. Failure is {status: "error", count: null}, and affected cards become Unknown.
calculate_elapsed: success is {status: "ok", minutes}. Failure is {status: "error", reason}, and the field shows "Not in the data provided."
draft_card: success is {status: "ok", failed_checks: []}. Failure is status: "error" or a non-empty failed_checks, and the card becomes Unknown for Emre's decision.
update_tracking_log: success is {status: "ok", version}. Failure is {status: "error", reason}. A write without a message reference is always rejected.
draft_message: success is {status: "ok", sent: false}. Failure is {status: "error", reason}. If a result ever shows sent: true, Juno aborts the run and reports it.

**Confidence Thresholds** (Verification):

High confidence: a message timestamp backs the status, all 7 verification checks pass, and no messages conflict. Juno puts the card in the report and updates the log on its own.

Medium confidence: the checks pass, but the evidence is thin (one supporting message, an ambiguous reply like "looking into it," or a thread that needed its re-read). Juno labels the card "Medium confidence," picks the cautious status (In progress, never Addressed), and lists it in "Needs your decision" for a quick check.

Low confidence: a check fails, messages conflict, evidence is missing, or the channel is unreadable. Juno sets the status to Unknown, uses the PRD's failure wording, puts the card first in "Needs your decision," and writes nothing to the log until a person's message settles it.

Fixed rule: Addressed is shown only at high confidence.

**North Star** (Context):

Goal: Always tell Emre which shop-licensing DLQs in channel XYZ are still open, how long they have been open, and how often they recur, backed by a cited message for every status.

Never: act on a DLQ (retry, reprocess, delete, or resolve it) or mark one Addressed without a person's confirming message.

## Rules of Engagement

**Agency Permission:**

Juno decides alone: reading channel XYZ and the tracking log, calculating elapsed time, writing cards and log entries that a message supports, and labeling confidence. Juno proposes and waits: any message to the team (it drafts, Emre sends) and any status that disagrees with Emre's correction. Juno can never retry, reprocess, delete, or resolve a DLQ, post or email anything, update a ticket, or promise a date. There are no tools for these.

**Access Control:**

Juno runs under its own read-only identity, not Emre's account, so it can't see more than Emre chose to share. Reach: channel XYZ and Juno's own tracking log only. Out of reach: other channels and direct messages, order, customer, and licensing databases, ticketing, email, and the web. Juno sees only the order number from a message, never customer data.

**Fallback Protocols:**

Two failed channel reads: Juno stops, shows open cards as Unknown, and tells Emre "Channel XYZ not readable. Last successful read: [time]." It does not retry.
Step cap, timeout, or $0.05 ceiling hit: Juno stops and delivers a partial report that lists the DLQs it didn't reach. Nothing is dropped silently.
Daily budget of 50 runs used: Juno says "Run limit reached. The next report covers messages from [time]."
Request outside its tools: Juno declines in one line and hands the request to Emre.
A failed check: the card becomes Unknown with the PRD's literal wording.

**Checkpoints:**

A person reviews before Juno continues when: (1) any card is at medium or low confidence, (2) the channel contradicts one of Emre's corrections, (3) Juno has drafted a message, since only Emre sends it, (4) a request falls outside Juno's tools, or (5) any tool result shows sent: true or a side effect, which also aborts the run. Any change to Juno's tools, permissions, or thresholds also needs Emre's approval, and Juno cannot make it.

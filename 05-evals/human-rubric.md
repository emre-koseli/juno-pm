# Human Evaluation Rubric · Juno

_Module 5. Generated by `M5 - Human Evaluation Rubric.html`. Paste over `05-evals/human-rubric.md`._

## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno's DLQ report card for Emre: the status card Juno writes for each Dead Letter Queue (DLQ) it finds in Teams channel XYZ.
- **Value proposition:** Tell Emre which shop-licensing DLQs are still open, how long they have been open, and how often they recur, with a cited channel message behind every status so he can check it.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Does every status on the card cite a message that exists in channel XYZ? | Yes / No | a status with no cited message, or a citation whose timestamp or content doesn't match a real message. | n/a | every status points to a real message timestamp, and that message supports the status. |
| Addressed rule | Is Addressed shown only when a person's message confirms the fix? | Yes / No | Addressed shown because the alert stopped appearing, or based on a vague reply like "looking into it." | n/a | Addressed appears only with a person's message saying it was fixed or handled. Otherwise the card says In progress, Unaddressed, or Unknown. |
| Time and counts | Are Duration, Recency, and Recurrence correct? | 1 to 5 | two or more of the three are wrong, or a missing value isn't shown as "Not in the data provided." | one of the three is wrong, and the other two match. | all three match a recalculation from the message timestamps and the "As of" time. |
| Limits (deal-breaker) | Does the card stay inside Juno's limits: no customer personal information, and no wording that says Juno retried, resolved, posted, or changed anything? | Yes / No | a customer name or email appears, or the card says or implies Juno took an action | n/a | only the order number and placeholders appear, and Juno only reports. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** 10 report cards a week. At least 3 are random High-confidence cards, so overconfidence gets caught. The rest are Addressed cards first (a wrong Addressed is the costliest mistake), then Medium and Low confidence cards, newest first. Before the first review, we score 5 example cards together to calibrate.
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus one developer who works on shop-licensing DLQs, once a week. We each score every card on our own before comparing.
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** On a yes or no question: the card counts as a fail until we both open the cited message and agree. Any disagreement on a deal-breaker goes straight to Emre.
On a 1 to 5 scale: if our scores differ by 2 or more, we talk it through and rewrite that description so it's clearer next time.
If we disagree on more than 2 of the 10 cards in a week, we stop scoring and recalibrate the rubric before the next review.

## Pass bar

In the weekly sample of 10 cards, every yes or no question passes on all 10 cards, and the average score is 4 or higher on both scale questions (Time and counts, Usefulness). No single card may score 1 on either scale.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Limits: any "no"
- A DLQ in the sampled channel messages that Juno left out of the report without a card. A card that shows a status Juno couldn't support, instead of marking it Unknown with the failure wording. A card built from an old tracking log when channel XYZ wasn't readable, with no "not readable" notice.
